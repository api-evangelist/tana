Sometimes your best ideas do not arrive during work hours. And the tasks you remember at 22:37 rarely survive until morning. Tana mobile now lets you capture to Tomorrow, giving you a simple, reliable way to offload thoughts the moment they come up and have them waiting for you in tomorrow's daily note when you start your day.
